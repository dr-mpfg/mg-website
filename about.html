Positions

  • Macro strategist and financial analyst, shipping industry, Greece (2026–).
  • Co-founder and Director, Garrison Sterling (2026–). Independent defence economics firm: a company-level data platform on the defence economy, with input-output and economic-impact models on top. Leads the modelling: economic-impact models and the supplier-level metrics built on public spend and company records.
  • Associate Researcher, Global Climate Forum, Berlin (2023–). OBFA-TRANSFORM: balance-sheet modelling of the euro-area monetary architecture, and delivery of the public Monetary Architecture Online Tool.
  • Senior Defence Economist, UKDSC, within the Ministry of Defence ecosystem (2025–2026). Input-Output and scenario models of the defence-industrial base; strategic briefings to government and industry. Reports I authored contributed to shaping MoD policy on measuring defence-industrial productivity, evaluating programme viability, assessing supply-chain resilience and vulnerability, and defining industrial readiness.
  • Lecturer in Economics, SOAS University of London (2023–2025).

Education

  • PhD, Economics, SOAS University of London (2020–2025). Thesis: Monetary Subordination in the Eurozone: Fragmentation and tiering of markets and institutions. Advisor: Prof. Costas Lapavitsas.
  • MSc, International Political Economy, LSE (2019–2020). Advisor: Prof. Jeffrey Chwieroth.
  • BSc, Economics (Hons), SOAS University of London (2016–2019). Winner of the Undergraduate Prize in Economics.

Methods

Modelling: balance-sheet mapping, stock-flow-consistent accounting, Input-Output, gravity models, VAR/SVAR, panel and time-series methods, scenario analysis.

Applied to: euro-area monetary policy and transmission, sovereign debt dynamics, fiscal-monetary interaction, financial fragmentation, shipping and trade finance, defence and war finance, trade and capital-flow regimes.

Tools: R and Python for macro-financial modelling and data work; Excel; LaTeX.

Recognition

Research Associate funding, OBFA-Transformation project (Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft and GCF Berlin). Scholarship, Minsky Summer Seminar, Levy Economics Institute of Bard College (2018).

Teaching at SOAS: Outstanding Teaching Award (2024–25), Best Use of Technology Enhanced Learning (2023–24), and a nomination for Postgraduate Research Supervisor of the Year (2024–25).

Personal

Dual Italian-British national, based in Greece. Italian (native) · English (bilingual) · French (advanced) · Greek and Spanish (beginner).
