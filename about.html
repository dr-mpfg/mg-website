About

The record: positions, training, published output, and where the full paper trail sits.

Macro strategist and financial analyst in the shipping industry. Co-founder and Director of Garrison Sterling, an independent defence economics firm, and Associate Researcher at the Global Climate Forum, Berlin, behind the balance-sheet models of the OBFA-TRANSFORM Project and the Monetary Architecture Online Tool. PhD in economics, SOAS University of London, on monetary subordination in the euro area, under Prof. Costas Lapavitsas; lecturer there to 2025; defence economist at the UKDSC, inside the Ministry of Defence ecosystem, to September 2026.

Interests and expertise

PhD-level macro-financial economist with expertise in sovereign risk, industrial strategy, geopolitical and defence economics, and eurozone monetary systems. Experience delivering applied economic analysis and strategic intelligence for governments and industry stakeholders, combining quantitative modelling, industrial analysis, and macro-financial research.

Built balance-sheet models, Input-Output models, sector productivity frameworks and industrial assessments used to inform policy and procurement discussions within the UK defence ecosystem. Co-founder of Garrison Sterling, a defence economics and intelligence firm linking firm-level defence money to macro-economic modelling, and author of Cargo & Collateral, a weekly publication on trade, freight and the credit beneath them. Published researcher with a track record in translating complex economic structures into decision-relevant analysis across policy, financial and strategic contexts.
