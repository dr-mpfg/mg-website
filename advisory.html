Advisory

Macro-financial strategy for institutions that need to know how a system clears before they need to know where it trades.

I take on commissioned work in whatever form the question needs: a short note, a full report, a model built to be interrogated, a dataset, or the data platform that holds it. The people I work for: shipping companies, ship-finance lenders and marine insurers; banks, asset managers and credit funds; central banks and public institutions. The method is the same for all of them: balance sheets first, who owes what to whom, funded how.

Areas

Shipping and trade finance

Freight exposure is working-capital exposure. Chartering and freight-rate risk, tonne-mile and throughput demand, forwarder and carrier working capital, trade finance structures, and the capital stack behind a fleet, including Greek and Mediterranean maritime economics. Written up in Cargo & Collateral.

Sovereign credit and monetary structure

Read at the level of the mechanism, not the spread. Sovereign risk and debt management, monetary transmission, collateral and repo, fragmentation and the settlement plumbing beneath it. Where the view comes from: the eurozone, subordination, macro-structure and DMOs.

Balance-sheet modelling

For questions that need a structure rather than a forecast. Bespoke modelling of a monetary architecture, a sector or a firm as interlocking balance sheets, built to be interrogated rather than believed. Method: balance-sheet modelling.
