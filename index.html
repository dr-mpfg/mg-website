Dr Matteo Giordano

Macro-financial strategist. Shipping, monetary structures, and off-balance-sheet finance.

Macro strategist, shipping industry·Co-founder and Director, Garrison Sterling·Associate Researcher, Global Climate Forum

Email Cargo & Collateral CV LinkedIn ORCID

Financial systems are claims and obligations: who owes what to whom, on whose balance sheet, funded how. Read that way, a rearmament programme is a working-capital cycle before it is a capability, and freight is a credit business wearing logistics clothes, where the credit is what breaks first. The method came out of the euro area: repo, collateral, TARGET, sovereign debt. It does not stay there.

In brief

Macro strategist and financial analyst in the shipping industry, based in Greece. Co-founder and Director of Garrison Sterling, an independent defence economics firm. Associate Researcher at the Global Climate Forum, Berlin, behind the balance-sheet models of the OBFA-TRANSFORM Project and the Monetary Architecture Online Tool.

PhD in economics, SOAS University of London: monetary subordination in the euro area, under Prof. Costas Lapavitsas; lecturer there to 2025. To September 2026, defence economist at the UKDSC, inside the Ministry of Defence ecosystem. Dual Italian-British national.

Four lines of work

  1. 1

    Shipping and trade finance

    The freight rate is the output; the credit chain is the mechanism. Chartering and freight rates, port throughput and tonne-mile demand, forwarder and carrier working capital, and the financing behind a single shipment, read from inside the industry rather than off a screen.

    Cargo & Collateral, the newsletter
  2. 2

    Macro-financial strategy and research

    Where the pressure sits in a system, and what it takes to move it. Commissioned structural reads for ship finance and freight, and for allocators and credit funds, on a sovereign, a sector or a market structure.

    Scope and terms
  3. 3

    Defence and security economics

    Capability is downstream of industrial structure and financing. The defence-industrial base read as an economy: where supply-chain and strategic dependencies concentrate, what industrial resilience actually requires, and whether sustained rearmament can be financed at all. The commercial side runs through Garrison Sterling.

    The research
  4. 4

    Monetary architecture and hierarchy

    Money is a hierarchy of interlocking balance sheets; a crisis is the moment they stop clearing. Collateral, repo, TARGET and subordination in the euro area, mapped that way, with the Global Climate Forum's OBFA-TRANSFORM project and its public Monetary Architecture tool.
